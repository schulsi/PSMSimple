# Reports

## At a Glance

In **Reports**, you record observations, damage, tasks, or notes related to fields. Reports can be filtered by status, type, and field. A report can include photos, coordinates, priority, and processing status.

Typical details:

- date
- field
- type
- priority
- status
- title and description
- coordinates
- photos

## How the Function Works

## Open reports

Open **Reports**. Filters are shown on the left, the report list in the middle, and details or the form on the right.

## Filter reports

You can filter reports by:

- **Status**, such as open, in progress, done, or discarded
- **Type**, such as wildlife damage, hail damage, disease, or pest
- **Field**

The list updates after a filter changes.

## Create a new report

1. Click **+ New Report**.
2. Select date, field, type, priority, and status.
3. Enter a short title.
4. Add a description.
5. Check or add coordinates.
6. Add photos if needed.
7. Click **Save**.

Date, type, and title are especially important so the report can be found later.

## Add photos

You can select images from the gallery or, if supported by the device, take a photo directly.

Photos are useful for:

- damage to vines, posts, or wire
- disease symptoms
- pest infestation
- hail or wildlife damage
- other field observations

## Edit a report

Select a report and click **Edit**. Change the required details and save the report again.

## Complete a report

Set the status to **Done** when the report has been handled. Use **Discarded** for entries that are no longer relevant.

## Common Issues

## No reports are visible

Check the filters. A selected status, type, or field may return no results.

## Camera is not available

Camera availability depends on device, browser, and permissions. Use **Select gallery** instead.

## Report cannot be edited

If edit or delete actions are not visible, you probably do not have write permissions.
